How many days can employees work remotely, and who approves exceptions?

Verified

Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week, provided their team maintains agreed coverage and the arrangement is documented with their manager .

Exceptions beyond three days require approval from both the employee's department head and People Operations. The policy recommends reviewing every exception after 90 days .
